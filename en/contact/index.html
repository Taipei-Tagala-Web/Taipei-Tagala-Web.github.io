Joining the club

We welcome those who share Rotary's ideal of service and want to take part in cultural inclusion work with immigrants and migrant workers. Membership usually begins by attending: come to three meetings as a guest, get to know the members and the service programme, then apply with a member's introduction. To find out more, message us through the club's Facebook page or ask a member you know to put you in touch.
