Meeting 228 2026.10.27 (Tue) Regular meeting

Ten Reasons PanSci Became a YouTuber

鄭國威 Founder and Chief Knowledge Officer, PanMedia Group; Host of PanSci

This meeting welcomes 鄭國威, one of Taiwan's best-known science communicators on YouTube, to share how PanSci moved from a website all the way to YouTube — using video, short-form video and diverse media formats to carry scientific knowledge beyond its own echo chamber and into content more people want to understand, share and discuss.

As founder and Chief Knowledge Officer of PanMedia Group and host of PanSci and Thought Experiment Lab, 鄭國威 has devoted his career to science communication, new media operations and the knowledge content industry, continuously exploring how expertise and public issues can adapt to a changing media landscape and reach more people's everyday lives.

Topics we'll explore

  • New challenges and openings in science communication
  • The move from website to video platform
  • Carrying science content beyond the echo chamber
