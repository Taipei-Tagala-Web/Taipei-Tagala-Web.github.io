Meeting 231 2026.12.08 (Tue) Regular meeting

Believe in Goodness: Stories from Migrant Workers to the Iron Brigade

潘威達 CEO, Eater Iron Bang F&B Group

How does a homegrown Taiwanese food-and-beverage group turn migrant worker talent into its own Iron Brigade? CEO 潘威達 of Eater Iron Bang F&B Group shares his talent philosophy in the restaurant industry and the guiding belief: believe in goodness, change lives with your life.

Founded in 2010, Eater Iron Bang now operates eleven restaurant brands — including Xiāng Lián Teppanyaki, Fù Wàng Hào, Jī Yóu Budget Teppanyaki, Wēi Yǔ Steakhouse, Zhōu Zhào Zi, Gān Mèi Lòng Táng and Yán Dì — with revenues exceeding NT$450 million in 2025. 潘威達 holds a doctorate in hospitality management from National Taiwan Normal University and has long taught there and at National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism. In June 2026 he launched the Believe in Goodness BE KIND industry-academia collaboration platform.

Topics we'll explore

  • Talent strategy for the F&B industry in an era of severe labour shortage
  • Long-term talent development and retention through industry-academia partnership
  • Deciding where to go as the group expands overseas
