Meeting 236 2027.01.12 (Tue) Regular meeting

Are You Being "Brainwashed" by Light?

陳示國 Professor, Dept. of Life Science; Professor, Institute of Brain and Mind Sciences, NTU

Scrolling your phone late at night — exhausted yet unable to sleep? Drowsy indoors during the day, wide awake once evening arrives? Light doesn't just let us see the world; it also quietly influences our brain, circadian rhythms, emotions, and social behaviour.

This meeting welcomes Dr. 陳示國, Professor at the Department of Life Science and the Institute of Brain and Mind Sciences, National Taiwan University, to introduce "non-image-forming vision" in neuroscience and explore how light shapes the brain and our health. Dr. Chen's research focuses on circadian rhythms, non-image-forming visual circuits, and neurobiology.

Topics we'll explore

  • The eye's third type of photoreceptor: Beyond sight — how the eye senses light and regulates the circadian clock
  • How light shapes the brain and health: sleep, memory, stress, emotion, and social impulses — all connected to environmental light
  • Using light wisely: What light, when? How to harness light for better sleep and well-being
